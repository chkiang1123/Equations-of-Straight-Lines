The delivery drone

A delivery drone flies in a perfectly straight line from the depot at (1, 2) to the drop point at (9, 8), with distances in kilometres. A straight river runs along the line y = 2x − 6, and the drone is not allowed to cross it below the town bridge.

Before you reach for a formula, spend a minute with the picture. This problem needs several ideas from different pages — spot them first.

What is actually on the grid?

Compare with what I noticed
  • Two fixed points: the depot (1, 2) and the drop point (9, 8).
  • One straight path joining them, sloping up to the right.
  • A second line, the river, steeper than the path — it is going up more sharply.
  • The two lines cross once, somewhere in the middle of the picture.
  • A halfway marker sits on the path, just before the crossing.
2Think

Which tools does this problem need, and in what order?

Compare with what I thought

The path is fixed by two points — so I can find its slope, then its equation. That is the two-point method.

The crossing is a point lying on both lines at once, so it is a pair of simultaneous equations: an intersection.

“How far did it fly” is a distance question, and “where is halfway” is a midpoint question.

So this one scenario quietly uses four different pages of the course. That is what a real exam question does.

★ Solutions

The drone problem, worked out

Check your three answers against these. If a method is unclear, the page names send you straight back.

Solution 1 — where does the drone cross the river?

Step 1. Equation of the path. Slope from (1, 2) to (9, 8):

m = 8 − 29 − 1 = 68 = 34

Using the point-slope form with (1, 2): y − 2 = 34(x − 1), so

y = 34x + 54

Step 2. Set the two equations equal, because the crossing lies on both:

34x + 54 = 2x − 6

Multiply by 4: 3x + 5 = 8x − 24, so 5x = 29 and x = 5.8. Then y = 2(5.8) − 6 = 5.6.

the drone crosses the river at (5.8, 5.6)

Revise: two-point form and intersections.

Solution 2 — how far did the drone fly?

Use the distance formula on the depot and the drop point:

AB = √((9 − 1)² + (8 − 2)²) = √(64 + 36) = √100 = 10

So it flies 10 km. Notice this is the same right-angled triangle as the slope: the run is 8 and the rise is 6.

Revise: prerequisites.

Solution 3 — had it passed the halfway point?

The midpoint of the journey is

(1 + 92, 2 + 82) = (5, 5)

The crossing is at (5.8, 5.6). Since 5.8 > 5, the drone has passed the halfway point by the time it reaches the river — but only just.
